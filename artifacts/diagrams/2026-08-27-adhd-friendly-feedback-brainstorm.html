More ADHD-friendly ways of giving feedback

Each branch: a pipeline stage, what the feedback loop already does for it, and candidate improvements with effort tags. Solid = exists; dashed = idea.

TL;DR

Capture is the bottleneck: today every piece of feedback demands a composed title + body at the exact moment attention is scarcest. The highest-leverage ideas make capture cost one tap or one utterance (optional title, per-unit verdicts, dictation, selection-prefill) and close the reward loop on the page itself.

Next → pick the ideas to turn into plans; cheapest compounding pair: optional title + selection-prefill.
